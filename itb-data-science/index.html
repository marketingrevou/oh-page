02 — Topik yang Akan Dibahas

Menjadi Data Scientist di Era AI

1

Data Analyst vs. Data Scientist: What Really Changes?

Explore how the scope and responsibility evolve—from reporting and explaining historical data to predicting future outcomes and owning data-driven decisions.

2

The 4 Key Gaps to Cross from Analyst to Data Scientist

Understand the capabilities that go beyond traditional analytics: statistical reasoning, predictive modelling, model validation, and turning analytical outputs into usable solutions.

3

How AI Is Changing the Data Science Career Path

Discover how AI can accelerate coding, analysis, and model development—and why human judgment, critical thinking, and the ability to validate AI-generated outputs are becoming even more important.

4

From Knowing Machine Learning to Applying It in Real Business Problems

Learn why knowing different algorithms is not enough, and what it takes to frame the right problem, choose and validate the right model, and understand when a model can actually be trusted.

5

Advancing Your Career in Data

Identify potential career paths beyond Data Analyst and understand what skills and responsibilities you need to develop to move toward Data Scientist and other advanced data roles.
